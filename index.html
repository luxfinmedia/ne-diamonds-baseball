The program

A program built on experience, passion, and player development

Our program was built on one mission: to develop complete ballplayers the right way. Located in North Jersey, we bring together a staff of former college standouts, all-state athletes, and leaders who’ve competed at the highest levels. Every coach on our team understands what it takes to succeed—because they’ve lived it.

From mechanics and fundamentals to baseball IQ and mindset, we teach the game with precision, detail, and a commitment to excellence. We create an environment where players can grow, build confidence, and develop the skills needed to play at the next level.

Our partners

The people behind the program

Strength training, nutrition, an indoor winter home, and the gear our players wear — four partners who make a year-round program possible.

The Academy

Speed, Strength & Power Training

Our organization has partnered with The Academy to give our athletes access to elite Speed, Strength, and Power training. Their science-based approach focuses on explosiveness, mobility, endurance, and overall athletic performance, helping players build the physical foundation needed to compete at higher levels. With experienced performance coaches and a structured development system, The Academy provides our players with the training environment required to stay strong, move efficiently, and perform at their best year-round.

Armor Nutrition

Athlete Meal & Wellness Support

We’ve partnered with Armor Nutrition to provide our athletes with customized meal plans, performance-focused nutrition guides, and practical wellness strategies. Their approach helps players fuel properly, recover faster, and develop healthy habits that support long-term athletic success. Armor Nutrition equips our players with the knowledge to stay energized, build strength, and perform at their highest level both on and off the field.

Cranford Performance Institute

Winter Training Partner

Our winter practices are held at Cranford Performance Institute, giving our athletes a professional indoor home for offseason development. This partnership allows us to run structured winter training sessions focused on mechanics, fundamentals, baseball IQ, and consistent reps regardless of weather. CPI’s high-quality indoor environment ensures players continue progressing throughout the offseason, entering the spring stronger, sharper, and more prepared.

Crispy NYC

Official Hype Wear Sponsor

We are proudly sponsored by Crispy NYC, a major hype wear and streetwear brand known for its bold style and growing cultural influence. Crispy generously donated premium shorts to every athlete in our program, adding a fresh, high-energy look that represents confidence, individuality, and attitude. Their support reflects their belief in youth development and the values our program stands for.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Ages, tournaments, practices, fees and travel — what families ask before they try out.

What age groups do you currently offer teams or training for?

We currently offer competitive travel teams and training for 8U, 9U, 10U, 11U, 12U, and 13U.

How many tournaments do your teams play each season?

Our teams typically compete in 4–5 tournaments per season, broken down as:

  • Spring: 4 tournaments
  • Summer: 4 tournaments
  • Fall: 4 tournaments
  • Winter: indoor training only (no tournaments)
Are you a year-round program or seasonal?

We operate as a year-round, tournament-based program. We do not participate in league play. Instead, we focus strictly on tournament development in the spring, summer, and fall, with skill-focused winter training.

Do you practice during the week, and how often?

Yes. We practice twice per week all year long, which includes weekly weekday practices and weekend tournaments during the competitive seasons.

Do you have an indoor facility for winter training?

Yes. We are officially partnered with Cranford Performance Institute (CPI), which provides indoor winter space for hitting, pitching, and all skill development.

What is included in the program or team fees?

Program fees typically cover:

  • Professional coaching
  • Field rentals
  • Tournament entry fees
  • Baseballs & training equipment
  • Indoor winter training at CPI
  • Administrative & program costs

Uniforms are purchased separately and are not included in team fees.

How far do you typically travel for tournaments?

Most tournaments take place 30–60 minutes from the Garwood / Union County area. Occasionally, teams may travel up to 90 minutes, depending on age group and competition level. We do not require overnight tournaments unless a team specifically chooses to participate in a special event.

What level of commitment is expected from players and families?

Northeast Diamonds is considered a first-priority program, and we expect families to treat it as such. Players are expected to:

  • Prioritize all team practices and tournaments
  • Communicate absences well in advance
  • Maintain a strong work ethic and positive attitude
  • Commit fully to year-round development
  • Show respect for teammates, coaches, and staff

Consistent participation is essential for both individual growth and team success.
